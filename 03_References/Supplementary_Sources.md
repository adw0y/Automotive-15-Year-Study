# 15-Year Longitudinal Automotive Study: Supplementary Sources & Benchmarks

This document records the official secondary and regulatory data sources used to corroborate, benchmark, and cross-validate figures extracted from primary annual corporate reports.

---

## 1. Regulatory & Exchange Rate Benchmarks

### 1.1 Reserve Bank of India (RBI) / Financial Benchmarks India Pvt. Ltd. (FBIL)
- **Document**: *Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy* & Daily/Monthly Reference Rate Bulletins.
- **Variables Supported**: Flow and stock exchange rate conversion series for Japanese Yen (JPY/INR) and South Korean Won (KRW/INR) from FY2010-11 to FY2024-25.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://www.rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Regulatory Source.

### 1.2 International Monetary Fund (IMF)
- **Document**: *International Financial Statistics (IFS) Database*.
- **Variables Supported**: Historical cross-currency exchange rate cross-checks and deflator verification.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://data.imf.org](https://data.imf.org)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Multilateral Source.

---

## 2. Industry Operational & Volume Registries

### 2.1 Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM)
- **Document**: *SIAM Flash Reports & Annual Industry Performance Circulars* (2010 to 2025).
- **Variables Supported**: V12 (ICE PV Sales), V14 (ICE/PV Market Share), V20 (EV PV Sales), and V21 (EV Penetration) for Indian operations of Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, and JSW MG Motor India.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://www.siam.in](https://www.siam.in)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Industry Registry.

### 2.2 Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association (JAMA)
- **Document**: *Motor Vehicle Statistics of Japan* (Annual editions FY2011 to FY2025).
- **Variables Supported**: Domestic production, export shares, and Kei-car segment volumes for Toyota Motor Corporation and Honda Motor Co., Ltd.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://www.jama.or.jp](https://www.jama.or.jp)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Industry Registry.

### 2.3 Korea Automobile & Mobility Association (KAMA)
- **Document**: *Korean Automotive Industry Statistics*.
- **Variables Supported**: Domestic sales, export splits, and eco-friendly vehicle registration breakdowns for Hyundai Motor Company.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://www.kama.or.kr](https://www.kama.or.kr)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Industry Registry.

---

## 3. Intellectual Property Databases

### 3.1 United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO)
- **Document**: *Patent Technology Monitoring Team (PTMT) Annual Patent Activity Reports*.
- **Variables Supported**: V03 (Patent Applications), V04 (Patent Stock), and V05 (Patents Granted) for US-registered portfolios of Toyota, Honda, Hyundai, Tata Motors/JLR, and Mahindra.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://www.uspto.gov](https://www.uspto.gov)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Patent Registry.

### 3.2 Japan Patent Office (JPO) & Korean Intellectual Property Office (KIPO)
- **Document**: *JPO Annual Status Report* & *KIPO Annual Report on Industrial Property*.
- **Variables Supported**: Cumulative domestic patent stock filings and grants.
- **Official Access Points**: [https://www.jpo.go.jp](https://www.jpo.go.jp) / [https://www.kipo.go.kr](https://www.kipo.go.kr)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Patent Registry.

---

## 4. Government & Institutional Filings (Special Cases)

### 4.1 Industrial Promotion & Investment Corporation of Odisha (IPICOL)
- **Document**: *State High-Level Clearance Authority (SHLCA) Approval Notifications - JSW Green Mobility Project (Feb 2024)*.
- **Variables Supported**: V17 (EV Investment Commitment) for JSW Group's ₹40,000 Crore Odisha integrated EV & 50 GWh battery project.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://investodisha.gov.in](https://investodisha.gov.in)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - State Regulatory Record.

### 4.2 Ministry of Heavy Industries (MHI), Government of India
- **Document**: *FAME-I, FAME-II, and Electric Mobility Promotion Scheme (EMPS) Gazette Notifications and Vehicle Deployment Dashboards*.
- **Variables Supported**: V20 (EV PV Sales) and V23 (EV Market Share) validation.
- **Official Access Point**: [https://heavyindustries.gov.in](https://heavyindustries.gov.in)
- **Classification**: Tier 2 - Official Government Gazette.
