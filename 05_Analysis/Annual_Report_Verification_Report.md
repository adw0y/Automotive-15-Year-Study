# 15-Year Longitudinal Automotive Study: Annual Report Numbers Verification & Source Audit

This document presents the audited verification of all historical financial and operational numbers compiled in `Automotive_15Year_Study_Master.xlsx` across the 15-year observation period (FY2010-11 to FY2024-25 / CY2010 to CY2024). Every observation is cross-referenced with its primary statutory annual report, Form 20-F SEC filing, or audited financial statements.

---

## 1. Audit Framework & Verification Protocol

### 1.1 Source Hierarchy
All observations adhere to a strict two-tier data lineage:
- **Tier 1 (Primary Audited Statutory Reports)**: Consolidated Annual Reports, SEC Form 20-F, K-IFRS Statutory Disclosures, and Directors' Reports published by the corporate issuers.
- **Tier 2 (Official Industry & Regulatory Registries)**: Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM), Reserve Bank of India (RBI) / FBIL reference exchange rates, Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) Vahan Portal, Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association (JAMA), and Korea Automobile & Mobility Association (KAMA).

### 1.2 Boundary & Disentanglement Rules
1. **Consolidated Financial Perimeter**: Group financial indicators—Revenue, R&D Expenses, Cash & Equivalents, Debt, Equity, Operating Cash Flow, Capex, EBIT, and ROCE—are extracted from consolidated audited financial statements to reflect full enterprise economic capacity.
2. **Passenger Vehicle (PV) Isolation**: Operational vehicle sales (ICE PV Sales, EV PV Sales, Market Share) strictly isolate passenger car and utility vehicle (SUV/MPV) operations. Commercial vehicles (buses, medium/heavy trucks, light commercial vehicles), farm equipment (tractors), and two/three-wheelers are stripped out.
3. **Currency Conversion Lineage**:
   - Japanese Yen (JPY) and South Korean Won (KRW) figures are converted to Indian Rupee (₹ Crore) using official RBI / FBIL annual rates.
   - **Flow items** (Revenue, R&D, Capex, OCF, EBIT) use period-average exchange rates.
   - **Stock items** (Cash, Total Assets, Total Debt, Total Equity) use fiscal year-end closing rates.
