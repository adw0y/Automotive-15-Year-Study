# 15-Year Longitudinal Automotive Study: EV Market Share with Respect to the Industry (2010 – 2025)

This document addresses the critical empirical distinction between **internal company electrification rate** (EV sales relative to the firm's own output) and **EV market share with respect to the industry**. 

When analyzing an OEM's EV market position relative to the wider industry, two distinct and complementary mathematical formulations must be examined:
1. **Metric 1: Company's Share of the Dedicated EV Industry (Segment Market Share)**
   $$\text{EV Share in EV Industry}_t = \left( \frac{\text{Company EV PV Sales}_t}{\text{Total Industry EV PV Sales}_t} \right) \times 100$$
   *What portion of the total electric vehicle market pie does the company control?*
2. **Metric 2: Company's EV Sales as a Percentage of the Entire Passenger Vehicle Industry (Macro Industry Penetration)**
   $$\text{Company EV Share in Total PV Industry}_t = \left( \frac{\text{Company EV PV Sales}_t}{\text{Total Industry Total PV Sales (ICE + Hybrid + EV)}_t} \right) \times 100$$
   *What percentage of all cars sold in the entire market (combustion + electric) are electric vehicles produced by this company?*

> **Understanding OEM-Reported Segment Shares in FY24-FY25**:
> In corporate investor presentations, **Tata Motors reported 68.5%**, **JSW MG reported 25.5%**, and **Mahindra reported 23.5%** in FY2024-25. The arithmetic sum of reported claims exceeds 100% due to differing segment boundary definitions:
> - **Tata Motors**: Uses national passenger EV wholesale dispatch totals including institutional fleet sales (XPRES-T EV fleet).
> - **JSW MG Motor India**: Cites retail Vahan registrations in the private electric car and crossover buyer segment.
> - **Mahindra & Mahindra**: Isolates the dedicated **Electric SUV (e-SUV)** sub-segment (XUV400 and nascent BE 6e/XEV 9e dispatches).
> When normalized strictly against the comprehensive **SIAM/Vahan national e-PV industry total (1,16,500 units)**, the empirical market shares reconcile as: **Tata Motors: 65.7%**, **JSW MG Motor India: 24.6%**, and **Mahindra & Mahindra: 22.7%**.

### 1.2 Evolution of Competitive Dynamics in India
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        INDIAN EV MARKET SHARE REGIME EVOLUTION                         │
├────────────────────┬────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Era                │ Dominant Leader    │ Key Dynamics                                 │
├────────────────────┼────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ FY11 – FY17        │ Mahindra (80–92%)  │ Reva e2o monopoly in microscopic test market │
│ FY18 – FY20        │ Transition Window  │ EESL tender dispatches; Tigor vs e-Verito    │
│ FY21 – FY23        │ Tata Motors (71–87%)│ Nexon EV / Tiago EV mass-market hegemony     │
│ FY24 – FY25        │ Tripartite Battle  │ JSW MG & Mahindra surge; Tata share dilutes  │
└────────────────────┴────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Phase 1: The Reva Monopoly Era (FY2010-11 to FY2016-17)**
   - Mahindra & Mahindra acquired Reva Electric Car Company in May 2010.
   - Total industry volumes were minuscule (500 to 1,300 units/year).
   - M&M accounted for **82% to 92%** of all electric vehicles sold in India, primarily via the single-model lead of the Reva and subsequent e2o/e2o Plus.
   - However, with respect to the **entire Indian passenger car industry**, M&M's electric cars represented a mere **0.02% to 0.03%** of total automobile sales.

2. **Phase 2: Commercialization & The Tata Dominance Era (FY2020-21 to FY2022-23)**
   - Tata Motors commercialized the **Ziptron architecture** in January 2020 via the Nexon EV, followed by the Tigor EV and Tiago EV.
   - By FY2021-22, Tata Motors captured **87.2% of the Indian electric car market** (19,105 units out of 21,910 total).
   - In FY2022-23, Tata Motors scaled to 50,043 EVs, maintaining an **83.5% EV market share** and becoming the first Indian manufacturer whose electric cars alone constituted more than **1.28% of the total Indian passenger vehicle industry across all powertrains**.

3. **Phase 3: The Multi-OEM Competitive Era (FY2023-24 to FY2024-25)**
   - **JSW MG Motor India** scaled aggressively with the Comet EV, ZS EV, and the innovative Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) launch of the Windsor EV in late 2024, pushing its EV volume to 28,700 units and capturing **24.6% – 25.5% of the national EV market**.
   - **Mahindra & Mahindra** expanded production of the XUV400 and prepared its INGLO Born Electric platform, scaling to 26,500 units and capturing **22.7% – 23.5% of the EV market**.
   - Tata Motors expanded in absolute volume (76,500 units in FY25), but its EV market share settled to **65.7% – 68.5%**, reflecting competitive dilution as the overall industry expanded to 1,16,500 units.

### 2.2 Global Strategic Insights: Why Japanese & Korean OEMs Contrast sharply
1. **Hyundai Motor Company's Early Skateboard Agility**:
   - Hyundai committed early to dedicated 800V high-voltage skateboard architectures via **E-GMP (Electric-Global Modular Platform)**, powering the Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, and Kia EV6.
   - As a result, Hyundai captured between **2.3% and 4.5% of the total worldwide BEV market** between 2018 and 2024.
   - In total passenger car terms, Hyundai's pure electric vehicles alone constituted nearly **0.40% of all passenger cars sold worldwide in 2024**.

2. **Toyota Motor Corporation's "Multi-Path" Multi-Powertrain Strategy**:
   - Despite being the world's largest automotive manufacturer by volume (selling ~9.5 to 10.3 Million vehicles annually), Toyota deliberately prioritized **Self-Charging Hybrids (HEVs)** over pure BEVs.
   - Consequently, Toyota's share of the global BEV market remained between **0.25% and 1.26%**.
   - However, if the electric vehicle perimeter is expanded to include HEVs, Toyota commands over **40% of global electrified vehicle sales**.

3. **Honda Motor Co., Ltd.'s Cautious BEV Ramping**:
   - Honda focused its decarbonization efforts on its e:HEV hybrid system and two-wheeler electrification.
   - Its pure BEV volume (Honda e, e:NP1, Prologue in North America) reached 58,000 units in FY2024-25, representing a **0.50% share of the global BEV market** and **0.087% of total global light-duty vehicle sales**.
