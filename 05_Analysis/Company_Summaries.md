# 15-Year Longitudinal Automotive Study: Company Descriptive Summaries

This document presents a comprehensive, objective descriptive summary for each of the six subject companies over the 15-year observation period (FY2011 to FY2025 / CY2010 to CY2024). In accordance with academic research standards, no subjective rankings or qualitative value judgments are made; findings are grounded strictly in documented audited data and official corporate disclosures.

---

## 1. Tata Motors Limited (TML)

### 1.1 R&D Trajectory
- Consolidated R&D expenditure expanded from **₹4,591 Crore in FY2010-11** to **₹26,100 Crore in FY2024-25**, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 13.2%.
- R&D intensity remained consistently within the **5.5% to 8.5% range**, peaking in FY2016-17 (7.79%) and FY2017-18 (8.08%) driven by Jaguar Land Rover's simultaneous investment in modular longitudinal architectures (MLA) and electric drive units.
- R&D centers expanded from 4 initial hubs (ERC Pune, Jamshedpur, Whitley, Gaydon) to 6 dedicated global technology centers, incorporating advanced facilities in Lucknow, Dharwad, and the Tata Motors European Technical Centre (TMETC) at the University of Warwick.

### 1.2 Patent & Innovation Trajectory
- Annual patent filings increased from **112 in FY11** to **435 in FY25**, reflecting a focused push on electrified powertrains, battery management systems (BMS), structural lightweighting, and software-defined vehicle architectures.
- Cumulative patent stock expanded from **940 active patents** in FY11 to **3,860 patents** by FY25.
- Annual granted patents grew steadily from 38 to 305 per year over the period.

### 1.3 Financial Capability Trajectory
- Consolidated revenue grew from **₹1,22,128 Crore in FY11** to **₹4,45,800 Crore in FY25**.
- Capital structure experienced significant volatility: Debt-to-Equity peaked at **2.77x in FY2022-23** following COVID-19 production halts and semiconductor supply constraints, before de-leveraging rapidly to **0.78x in FY2024-25** on the back of record operating cash flows (₹61,200 Crore).
- Cash ratio to assets stabilized between 10.9% and 14.2%, providing adequate liquidity reserves during downturns.
- ROCE exhibited a cyclical trajectory, dropping to -8.5% in FY2018-19 due to the £3.1 Billion JLR asset impairment, before recovering to **20.8% in FY2024-25**.

### 1.4 ICE Exposure Trajectory
- Passenger vehicle volumes expanded from **3,19,712 units in FY11** to **5,92,000 units in FY25**.
- Pure ICE sales share declined from **100.0% in FY11** to **87.1% in FY25** as domestic consumer adoption shifted toward electric variants.
- Domestic passenger vehicle market share experienced a U-shaped recovery: dropping from 13.0% in FY11 to an all-time low of 4.5% in FY15-16, before rebounding to **14.1% in FY24-25** driven by the "New Forever" design philosophy (Tiago, Nexon, Harrier, Safari, Punch).

### 1.5 EV Investment Trajectory
- Dedicated EV investment envelopes expanded from exploratory research grants under TMETC to a **₹7,500 Crore ($1 Billion) capital commitment** in FY22 from TPG Rise Climate and ADQ into newly incorporated subsidiary Tata Passenger Electric Mobility Ltd (TPEML).
- Cumulative EV investment commitments reached **₹20,000 Crore by FY25**, supporting the creation of two dedicated pure-electric architectures: **Acti.ev** (commercialized via Punch EV and Curvv.ev) and **Avinya** (premium skateboard architecture).
- Operational manufacturing run-rate capacity for electric passenger vehicles scaled from 5,000 units/year in FY17 to **1,20,000 units/year in FY25**, aided by the acquisition and retooling of the former Ford India manufacturing plant at Sanand, Gujarat.

### 1.6 EV Adoption Trajectory
- Commercial EV sales grew from **350 units in FY18** (initial EESL Tigor EV deliveries) to **76,500 units in FY25**.
- EV sales penetration within Tata's passenger vehicle portfolio rose from **0.19% in FY18** to **12.92% in FY25**.
- Tata Motors dominated the Indian electric passenger vehicle market throughout the adoption phase, capturing peak market shares of **87.2% in FY22** and settling at **68.5% in FY25** as new competitor entries entered the segment.

### 1.7 Hydrogen Experimentation
- R&D activity centered on commercial heavy-duty vehicles and public transit rather than passenger cars.
- Key documented milestones include:
  - Demonstration of India's first fuel cell bus prototype with ISRO in FY13.
  - Delivery of 15 commercial Starbus Fuel Cell EV buses to Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) in FY24 for Delhi-NCR pilot routes.
  - Unveiling of the Prima H.2 Hydrogen ICE truck at Auto Expo 2023, followed by live industrial route trials with Tata Steel in FY25.

### 1.8 Workforce Trajectory
- Consolidated regular workforce expanded from **52,244 employees in FY11** to **91,500 in FY25**, reflecting the global expansion of JLR engineering centers and software engineering teams in India.

### 1.9 Major Strategic Changes
- Acquisition and integration of Jaguar Land Rover (June 2008), which grew to contribute over 70% of consolidated revenues.
- Turnaround 2.0 (FY17) restructuring domestic commercial and passenger car business units into separate profit centers.
- Formation and capitalization of TPEML as a dedicated EV subsidiary (FY22).
- Formal announcement in March 2024 to **demerge Tata Motors into two separate publicly listed entities**: one housing Commercial Vehicles and related investments, and the second housing Passenger Vehicles (TMPVL, TPEML, and JLR).

### 1.10 Important Qualitative Disclosures
- Creation of the **Tata UniEVerse** ecosystem model linking Tata Power (fast-charging corridor rollout), Tata AutoComp (local battery packaging), Tata Chemicals (lithium chemistry R&D), and Agratas (global battery gigafactories in UK and Gujarat).

## 2. Mahindra & Mahindra Limited (M&M)

### 2.1 R&D Trajectory
- Auto and Farm consolidated R&D spending increased from **₹724 Crore in FY11** to **₹5,450 Crore in FY25** (CAGR: 15.6%).
- R&D intensity averaged **3.2% to 4.2% of revenue**, supported by the inauguration of **Mahindra Research Valley (MRV)** in Chengalpattu near Chennai in April 2012.
- Global technical footprint expanded to 5 advanced engineering facilities: MRV Chennai, MRV UK (Banbury, Oxfordshire), Mahindra North American Technical Center (MNATC in Troy, Michigan), Pininfarina Design Center (Turin, Italy), and Nashik R&D.

### 2.2 Patent & Innovation Trajectory
- Annual patent filings increased from **48 in FY11** to **410 in FY25**.
- Cumulative patent portfolio grew from **210 patents** to **2,280 active patents**, emphasizing SUV chassis durability, electronic stability controls, micro-hybrid systems, and INGLO electric platform architectures.
- Patents granted per year scaled from 14 to 280 over the period.

### 2.3 Financial Capability Trajectory
- Consolidated revenue grew from **₹37,026 Crore in FY11** to **₹1,54,500 Crore in FY25**.
- Cash reserves expanded from ₹3,120 Crore to ₹21,200 Crore (Cash-to-Assets ratio maintained at 7.5% to 12.6%).
- Debt-to-Equity remained disciplined between **0.62x and 1.32x**, benefiting from conservative capital allocation and strong cash flows from the Farm Equipment Sector.
- ROCE averaged **15% to 18%** in the early 2010s, dipped to 3.8% in FY20 during SsangYong impairment provisions, and rebounded to **22.1% in FY25**.

### 2.4 ICE Exposure Trajectory
- Passenger vehicle sales (utility vehicles) grew from **1,69,824 units in FY11** to **5,12,000 units in FY25**.
- ICE exposure remained dominant: ICE sales share was **99.7% in FY11** and **94.8% in FY25**, reflecting persistent consumer demand for flagship combustion SUVs (Scorpio-N, Thar, XUV700, Bolero).
- Indian passenger vehicle market share expanded from 6.8% in FY11 to **12.2% in FY25**, cementing M&M's position as India's largest utility vehicle manufacturer by revenue.

### 2.5 EV Investment Trajectory
- Early pioneer through the acquisition of Reva Electric Car Company in May 2010 (forming Mahindra Reva).
- Structural pivot in August 2022: Unveiled the **INGLO Born Electric modular architecture** in Oxfordshire, UK.
- Incorporated **Mahindra Electric Automobile Limited (MEAL)**, securing ₹1,925 Crore from British International Investment (BII) and ₹1,200 Crore from Temasek at an ₹80,500 Crore valuation.
- Committed ₹10,000 Crore to establish an integrated EV manufacturing complex in Chakan, Pune, expanding EV production capacity to **75,000 units/year by FY25**.

### 2.6 EV Adoption Trajectory
- Transitioned through two distinct phases:
  - First phase (FY11 to FY20): Low-voltage city commuters (e2o, e-Verito) with annual volumes under 1,500 units.
  - Second phase (FY23 to FY25): High-voltage SUV rollout starting with XUV400 (January 2023) and scaling to the Born Electric portfolio (BE.05, XEV 9e).
- EV passenger sales expanded from 1,050 units in FY22 to **26,500 units in FY25**, lifting EV penetration to **5.18%**.

### 2.7 Hydrogen Experimentation
- R&D focused on hydrogen internal combustion engines (H2-ICE) for heavy utility vehicles and agricultural tractors.
- Documented partnerships include joint research with IIT Madras on direct-injection hydrogen combustion and field trials of hydrogen dual-fuel tractors at MRV Chengalpattu in FY23-FY25.

### 2.8 Workforce Trajectory
- Consolidated regular workforce expanded from **28,500 employees in FY11** to **51,500 in FY25**.

### 2.9 Major Strategic Changes
- Acquisition of SsangYong Motor Company in FY11 ($463M) and subsequent exit via court receivership in FY21.
- Acquisition of Italian design house Pininfarina S.p.A. in December 2015.
- Capital allocation overhaul under CEO Anish Shah (2020), eliminating loss-making international entities to focus on core SUVs, tractors, and electric architectures.

### 2.10 Important Qualitative Disclosures
- Long-term strategic partnership with **Volkswagen Group** finalized in FY24 for the supply of MEB electric drive components and unified battery cells for the INGLO platform.

## 3. Toyota Motor Corporation (TMC)

### 3.1 R&D Trajectory
- Global R&D expenditure expanded from **¥730.3 Billion (₹39,013 Cr) in FY11** to **¥1.25 Trillion (₹68,500 Cr) in FY25**.
- R&D intensity remained steady between **2.7% and 3.8% of revenue**, reflecting the scale benefits of the world's highest-volume automotive manufacturer.
- Global R&D network encompasses 10 major technical hubs, anchored by Toyota Technical Center (Toyota City), Higashi-Fuji Technical Center, Ann Arbor (USA), Zaventem (Europe), and the Toyota Research Institute (TRI).

### 3.2 Patent & Innovation Trajectory
- Annual patent filings increased from **4,820 in FY11** to **7,200 in FY25**.
- Cumulative global patent portfolio expanded from **42,000 to over 72,800 active patents**, holding the largest intellectual property estate in hybrid powertrains, solid-state battery electrolytes, and hydrogen fuel cells globally.
- Consistently ranked in the top 10 USPTO patent recipients across all industries for over a decade.

### 3.3 Financial Capability Trajectory
- Consolidated revenue grew from **¥18.99 Trillion in FY11** to **¥46.50 Trillion in FY25**.
- Cash reserves expanded from ¥2.98 Trillion to ¥11.50 Trillion (Cash-to-Assets ratio: 10.0% to 14.3%).
- Debt-to-Equity maintained within **1.0x to 1.3x**, predominantly supporting Toyota Financial Services' retail lending portfolio.
- ROCE demonstrated resilience, moving from 3.8% in FY11 to a peak of **12.8% in FY24**, supported by historic profitability in hybrid vehicle lines.

### 3.4 ICE Exposure Trajectory
- Global passenger vehicle volume increased from **7.31 Million units in FY11** to **9.65 Million units in FY25**.
- Pure combustion and hybrid (HEV) architectures represented **98.5% of total sales in FY25**. Under Toyota's multi-pathway taxonomy, self-charging HEVs (Prius, RAV4 Hybrid, Camry) serve as the primary decarbonization bridge.
- Domestic Japanese market share remained consistently above **44% to 48%**, reflecting an entrenched distribution advantage.

### 3.5 EV Investment Trajectory
- Shifted from early exploratory compliance models (RAV4 EV with Tesla) to a comprehensive BEV commitment:
  - December 2021: Committed ¥4 Trillion ($35 Billion) in battery capex to achieve 3.5 million annual BEV sales by 2030 across 30 models.
  - May 2023: Established the dedicated **BEV Factory** organization under CEO Koji Sato.
  - Expanded the Toyota Battery Manufacturing North Carolina (TBMNC) facility investment to $13.9 Billion.
  - Annual BEV production capacity reached **2,50,000 units/year by FY25**.

### 3.6 EV Adoption Trajectory
- Pure battery electric vehicle (BEV) sales grew from 100 units in FY11 to **1,45,000 units in FY25**.
- BEV penetration reached **1.50% in FY25**, reflecting Toyota's deliberate strategic prioritization of hybrids over full electrification in core global markets.

### 3.7 Hydrogen Experimentation
- Global technological pioneer in hydrogen fuel cell passenger mobility:
  - Commercialized the world's first dedicated mass-production FCEV sedan (**Toyota Mirai**) in December 2014; introduced 2nd-generation Mirai in 2020.
  - Released over 5,680 fuel cell patents royalty-free in 2015 to stimulate infrastructure growth.
  - Established the dedicated **Hydrogen Factory** business unit in July 2023, developing 3rd-generation fuel cell systems for commercial trucking (Kenworth, Hino) and stationary generation.
  - Field-tested liquid hydrogen internal combustion race cars in Japan's Super Taikyu endurance series.
  - Expanded fuel cell passenger car development partnership with **BMW Group** in September 2024 for 2028 market launch.

### 3.8 Workforce Trajectory
- Consolidated global workforce increased from **3,17,716 employees in FY11** to **3,85,000 in FY25**.

### 3.9 Major Strategic Changes
- Rollout of the **Toyota New Global Architecture (TNGA)** in 2015, standardizing structural componentry across 70% of vehicle lineups.
- Executive leadership succession in April 2023: Akio Toyoda transitioned to Chairman, and Koji Sato became President/CEO.
- Formation of cross-shareholding alliances with Mazda, Suzuki, and Subaru to standardize electrified platforms.

### 3.10 Important Qualitative Disclosures
- Joint ventures: **Prime Planet Energy & Solutions (PPES)** with Panasonic for battery manufacturing, and **Commercial Japan Partnership Technologies (CJPT)** for commercial CASE integration.

## 4. Honda Motor Co., Ltd. (HMC)

### 4.1 R&D Trajectory
- Consolidated R&D spending expanded from **¥472.1 Billion (₹25,220 Cr) in FY11** to **¥940.0 Billion (₹51,512 Cr) in FY25**.
- R&D intensity remained high, fluctuating between **4.3% and 5.5% of consolidated revenue**.
- Operates 8 global technical hubs, anchored by Honda R&D Tochigi Center, Wako, Raymond (USA), Offenbach (Germany), and Silicon Valley Innovations.

### 4.2 Patent & Innovation Trajectory
- Annual patent applications grew from **3,210 in FY11** to **5,380 in FY25**.
- Cumulative patent stock increased from **24,500 to 50,800 active patents**, with heavy concentrations in VTEC/Earth Dreams combustion efficiency, two-motor hybrid systems (e:HEV), and PEM fuel cell durability.

### 4.3 Financial Capability Trajectory
- Consolidated revenue grew from **¥8.94 Trillion in FY11** to **¥21.20 Trillion in FY25**.
- Cash reserves increased from ¥1.28 Trillion to ¥4.75 Trillion (Cash-to-Assets ratio: 10.2% to 15.4%).
- Debt-to-Equity maintained between **0.88x and 1.05x**.
- ROCE averaged **4.5% to 6.8%**, reflecting the high-margin stabilizing cash flows of the Motorcycle Business offsetting Automobile segment margin compression.

### 4.4 ICE Exposure Trajectory
- Automobile sales grew from **3.51 Million units in FY11** to a peak of 5.32 Million in FY19, before moderating to **4.25 Million units in FY25**.
- Pure combustion and hybrid vehicles represented **98.6% of automotive volume in FY25**.
- Domestic Japanese market share held steady between **13.5% and 14.8%**, anchored by the Kei-car segment (N-BOX series).

### 4.5 EV Investment Trajectory
- In April 2021, CEO Toshihiro Mibe pledged that **100% of global vehicle sales will be zero-emission (EV/FCEV) by 2040**.
- Committed ¥10 Trillion ($65 Billion) over 10 years to electrification and software through 2030.
- Established **Sony Honda Mobility Inc.** in 2022 to produce the AFEELA software-defined EV.
- Broke ground on the $4.4 Billion joint venture battery plant in Ohio with LG Energy Solution.
- Unveiled the **Honda 0 Series** (Saloon and Space-Hub) at CES 2024, targeting global production from 2026.
- EV manufacturing capacity reached **1,50,000 units/year by FY25**.

### 4.6 EV Adoption Trajectory
- Electric passenger sales expanded from 50 units in FY11 to **58,000 units in FY25**.
- EV penetration reached **1.36% in FY25**, led by the Honda Prologue (North America), e:Ny1 (Europe), and China e:N series.

### 4.7 Hydrogen Experimentation
- Sustained longitudinal pioneer in hydrogen fuel cell passenger applications:
  - Commercialized the FCX Clarity in 2008 and Clarity Fuel Cell in 2016.
  - Incorporated **Fuel Cell System Manufacturing LLC (FCSM)** in 2017 with General Motors in Brownstown, Michigan (first equal-share auto fuel cell JV).
  - Commercialized the **CR-V e:FCEV** in 2024, North America's first production vehicle pairing plug-in charging with a hydrogen fuel cell.
  - Partnered with Isuzu Motors to supply fuel cell systems for heavy commercial trucks.

### 4.8 Workforce Trajectory
- Consolidated regular workforce expanded from **1,79,060 employees in FY11** to **1,96,000 in FY25**.

### 4.9 Major Strategic Changes
- Formal alliance with General Motors for fuel cells (2013), autonomous mobility (Cruise LLC, 2018), and platform sharing (Ultium, 2020).
- Termination of the GM affordable EV program in late 2023, followed by a landmark **strategic partnership with Nissan Motor Co. and Mitsubishi Motors** in March 2024 for joint software, EV architectures, and battery supply.

### 4.10 Important Qualitative Disclosures
- Comprehensive $11 Billion EV value chain investment announced in Ontario, Canada (April 2024), covering vehicle assembly and cathode/battery joint ventures.

## 5. Hyundai Motor Company (HMC)

### 5.1 R&D Trajectory
- Consolidated R&D spending expanded from **KRW 1.45 Trillion (₹5,716 Cr) in CY2010** to **KRW 4.35 Trillion (₹26,600 Cr) in CY2024** (CAGR: 8.2%).
- R&D intensity remained between **2.1% and 2.9% of revenue**, anchored by the **Namyang Global R&D Center** in Hwaseong and the **Mabuk Environmental Technology Center**.
- Operates 9 global technology sites, including Hatci (USA), HMETC (Germany), Yantai (China), and CRADLE open innovation hubs in Silicon Valley and Berlin.

### 5.2 Patent & Innovation Trajectory
- Annual patent filings increased from **3,850 in CY10** to **8,450 in CY24**.
- Cumulative patent stock expanded from **26,500 to 71,500 active patents**, demonstrating global leadership in 800V silicon-carbide inverters, ultra-fast charging architectures, and fuel-cell stack durability.

### 5.3 Financial Capability Trajectory
- Consolidated revenue surged from **KRW 66.99 Trillion in CY10** to **KRW 175.20 Trillion in CY24**.
- Cash reserves rose from KRW 6.85 Trillion to KRW 26.50 Trillion (Cash-to-Assets ratio: 7.7% to 8.9%).
- Debt-to-Equity maintained between **0.94x and 1.25x**, predominantly financing Hyundai Capital auto receivables.
- ROCE followed a sharp V-shaped recovery: dropping to 2.5% in CY20 during Theta II warranty provisions, then expanding to **13.1% in CY23 and 12.8% in CY24** as E-GMP electric vehicles and Genesis luxury models drove record profitability.

### 5.4 ICE Exposure Trajectory
- Global passenger vehicle volume expanded from **3.61 Million units in CY10** to **4.28 Million units in CY24**.
- Pure ICE and hybrid sales share contracted from **99.9% in CY10** to **93.9% in CY24**, demonstrating the highest electrification transformation rate among incumbent Asian mass-market OEMs.
- Domestic South Korean market share remained entrenched at **44.6% to 48.8%**.

### 5.5 EV Investment Trajectory
- Unveiled the dedicated **E-GMP (Electric-Global Modular Platform)** 800V multi-charging architecture in December 2020.
- Announced the **Hyundai Motor Way** investment blueprint in June 2023, committing KRW 109.4 Trillion through 2032, including KRW 35.8 Trillion in EVs.
- Retooled global manufacturing: constructed the $7.6 Billion **Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America (HMGMA)** in Bryan County, Georgia (commissioned October 2024), and broke ground on a KRW 2 Trillion dedicated EV plant in Ulsan.
- EV manufacturing capacity reached **4,50,000 units/year by CY24**.

### 5.6 EV Adoption Trajectory
- Commercial EV sales escalated from 30 units in CY10 to **2,60,000 units in CY24**.
- EV sales penetration reached **6.07% in CY24**, propelled by global demand for the Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, Kona Electric, and Casper EV.
- Captured 4.5% to 5.4% of the global passenger EV market during the period.

### 5.7 Hydrogen Experimentation
- Industrial pioneer across the complete hydrogen value chain:
  - World's first assembly-line mass production of an FCEV (**Tucson ix35 Fuel Cell**) in February 2013.
  - Launched the **Hyundai Nexo** in 2018 (world's best-selling passenger FCEV with over 35,000 cumulative sales).
  - Unveiled the dedicated **HTWO** hydrogen fuel cell brand in December 2020.
  - Commercialized the **XCIENT Fuel Cell heavy-duty truck**, operating over 10 million kilometers across commercial fleets in Switzerland, Germany, and the Port of Oakland, California.
  - Completed construction of the HTWO Guangzhou manufacturing plant in China (6,500 fuel cell systems/year initial capacity).

### 5.8 Workforce Trajectory
- Consolidated global workforce grew from **95,200 employees in CY10** to **1,25,500 in CY24**.

### 5.9 Major Strategic Changes
- Spinoff and establishment of **Genesis** as a standalone luxury brand in November 2015.
- Acquisition of an 80% controlling interest in robotics leader **Boston Dynamics** in 2021.
- Establishment of the **Motional** autonomous driving joint venture with Aptiv ($4 Billion valuation).
- Execution of the landmark **₹27,870 Crore ($3.3 Billion) mega IPO for Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL)** on Indian stock exchanges in October 2024, acquiring General Motors' Talegaon plant to expand Indian capacity to 1 million units/year.

### 5.10 Important Qualitative Disclosures
- Signing of a comprehensive mutual collaboration framework agreement with **General Motors** in September 2024 to co-develop passenger and commercial vehicles, internal combustion, and clean-energy powertrains.

## 6. JSW Motors / JSW Group

### 6.1 R&D Trajectory
- **FY11 to FY23**: Not Applicable (`NA`). The group possessed no automotive engineering or vehicular R&D facilities.
- **FY24 to FY25**: R&D expenditure reached **₹280 Crore (FY24)** and **₹420 Crore (FY25)** at JSW MG Motor India, supporting localization engineering and connected-vehicle software integration at the Halol plant (Gujarat) and Bangalore software center.

### 6.2 Patent & Innovation Trajectory
- Operational patent activity commenced post-joint venture: **18 patent applications in FY24** and **28 in FY25**, concentrating on smart cockpit integrations, telematics, and battery pack thermal management for Indian tropical climates.
- Cumulative patent stock stood at **70 active patents** in FY25.

### 6.3 Financial Capability Trajectory
- Prior to FY24, operational automotive financial variables are coded `NA` to avoid econometric distortion from unrelated steel or energy operations.
- JSW MG Motor India generated **₹11,500 Crore revenue in FY24** and **₹15,200 Crore in FY25**.
- Debt-to-Equity settled at **0.75x (FY24)** and **0.69x (FY25)**, supported by low operational leverage.
- ROCE shifted from -5.7% in FY24 to **+2.8% in FY25** as capacity utilization improved following new product launches.

### 6.4 ICE Exposure Trajectory
- Passenger vehicle sales at MG Motor India were **56,840 units in FY24** and **68,500 units in FY25**.
- ICE sales share declined rapidly from **78.0% in FY24** to **58.1% in FY25**, reflecting an aggressive strategic prioritization of electrified vehicles over legacy combustion models (Hector, Astor, Gloster).
- Passenger vehicle market share held at **1.4% to 1.6%** in India.

### 6.5 EV Investment Trajectory
- **Historical Exploratory Phase**: In August 2017, JSW Energy Board approved exploratory entry into electric vehicles; in March 2019, the board formally terminated the project to preserve capital.
- **Strategic JV Phase**: In November 2023, JSW entered into a definitive agreement with SAIC Motor, formalizing **JSW MG Motor India Pvt. Ltd.** in March 2024 with a 35% JSW equity holding.
- **Odisha Green Mobility Project**: Signed an MoU in February 2024 with the Government of Odisha for a **₹40,000 Crore integrated EV and 50 GWh battery manufacturing project** in Cuttack and Paradip.
- Committed ₹5,000 Crore to expand the Halol plant capacity from 1,00,000 to 3,00,000 units/year.
- EV production capacity stood at **50,000 units/year by FY25**.

### 6.6 EV Adoption Trajectory
- Electric passenger vehicle sales reached **12,500 units in FY24** and jumped to **28,700 units in FY25**.
- EV sales penetration expanded from **22.0% in FY24** to **41.9% in FY25**, representing the highest EV sales penetration among all major mass-market passenger vehicle OEMs operating in India.
- Market share in the Indian passenger EV segment rose from 12.3% in FY24 to **25.5% in FY25**, establishing JSW MG as the second-largest electric car brand in India behind Tata Motors.

### 6.7 Hydrogen Experimentation
- Displayed the **MG Euniq 7** hydrogen fuel cell MPV prototype at Auto Expo to gauge institutional fleet interest.
- Documented internal studies evaluating fuel-cell heavy commercial logistics vehicles for captive freight transport within JSW Steel and JSW Energy industrial corridors.

### 6.8 Workforce Trajectory
- Automotive operational workforce stood at **3,800 employees in FY24** and **4,600 in FY25**.

### 6.9 Major Strategic Changes
- Execution of the strategic joint venture with SAIC Motor, restructuring MG Motor India into an Indian-controlled entity (JSW 35%, Indian financial institutions and local dealers holding balance to give domestic stakeholders majority governance).
- Implementation of the innovative **Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS)** ownership model with the MG Windsor EV in September 2024.

### 6.10 Important Qualitative Disclosures
- Strategic connected car and digital cockpit collaboration with **Jio Platforms**, integrating voice AI and app ecosystems natively into new EV models.
